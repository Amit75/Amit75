# Aarulya Store international country-pack framework

There is no universal "international approval". Each target country requires its own reviewed pack before commercial launch.

## Per-country required evidence

Every country pack must identify:

- ISO country code and pack version;
- verified legal operator and public contact details;
- applicable privacy/data-protection requirements;
- hosting, residency and cross-border-transfer decision;
- consumer terms, cancellation/refund rules where relevant;
- tax, invoice and payment obligations where relevant;
- age, child-safety and advertising requirements;
- content/distribution restrictions;
- security/incident reporting obligations;
- support and dispute-handling channel;
- effective date and legal/professional review evidence;
- owner approval tied to the exact pack digest.

## Default rule

A country that has no reviewed pack is blocked. A framework file, translation or generic policy does not authorize launch in that country.

The international country-pack gate remains PARTIAL while the framework exists but no individual country is launch-approved.
