This page is source-ready but is not an active production policy. It becomes effective only after the verified legal operator, effective date and required launch approvals are activated.

Privacy notice

Product: Aarulya Store · Privacy contact: privacy@aarulya.com

Initial release defaults

  • Advertising is disabled for the initial release.
  • Analytics are disabled until a consented and privacy-reviewed implementation is separately approved.
  • No permission prompt is intended on first launch; permissions are requested only when a feature needs them.
  • Cross-border transfer remains blocked by default until a reviewed country/hosting decision exists.

Data categories

The production service may process account identifiers, catalog/version metadata, download/install/update receipts, device-compatible aggregate telemetry where approved, security/publication evidence, and support/audit records. Production activation must bind every category to a documented purpose and retention rule.

Choice, export and deletion

Optional consent must be declineable. Export and verified deletion workflows are release gates and must be tested before the related processing is enabled. Requests can be directed to privacy@aarulya.com after production activation.

Children and sensitive data

Child-directed processing, sensitive-data processing, behavioral advertising to children and precise-location use require separate review. Features that have not completed that review remain disabled.

Processors and changes

No processor or analytics service is approved merely by appearing in source. The production processor list, hosting region, notice version and effective date must be reviewed and published before activation.
