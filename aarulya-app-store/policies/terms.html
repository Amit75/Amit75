This page is source-ready but is not an active production policy. It becomes effective only after the verified legal operator, effective date and required launch approvals are activated.

Terms of use

Product: Aarulya Store · Support: support@aarulya.com

Current distribution boundary

  • The initial Store is restricted to original Aarulya-owned applications that pass the Store release gate.
  • Third-party app submissions are disabled for the initial release.
  • Imported, copied, modified or re-signed third-party APKs are prohibited.
  • Android installation always requires user-visible confirmation; silent installation is not provided.

Release availability

A catalog entry does not mean an APK is available. Download remains blocked until the exact package, version, digest, signer and evidence record are approved. A release can be withdrawn or blocked when integrity, security, rights or recovery evidence is missing or revoked.

User responsibilities

Users must keep account credentials private, follow device security prompts and avoid tampering with download or verification mechanisms. Security concerns can be reported to security@aarulya.com.

Rights and complaints

Copyright or ownership concerns can be sent to copyright@aarulya.com. No final contractual limitation, governing-law clause or dispute term becomes active from this draft; those items require verified operator details and legal review at launch.
