Pre-launch draft

This page is source-ready but is not an active production policy. It becomes effective only after the verified legal operator, effective date and required launch approvals are activated.

Pricing and billing

Commercial contact is activated only with the verified production operator and billing configuration.

Initial Store build

There is no live payment collection in this source build. Consumer catalog browsing and first-party distribution are not represented here as an active paid subscription.

Paid services

Enterprise private-store, managed-release, implementation or support offerings may be introduced only after pricing, tax/invoice, payment-provider, refund/cancellation and legal-review gates are completed. A quote, invoice, contract or pipeline record is not treated as settled revenue.

No hidden activation

Source code, a catalog listing or a deployment does not activate billing. Live payment flags remain false until the approved production billing configuration and owner approval evidence are present.
