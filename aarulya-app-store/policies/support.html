Support policy

General: support@aarulya.com · Security: security@aarulya.com · Privacy: privacy@aarulya.com

Service status

No contractual SLA is active in this pre-launch build. Public launch requires mailbox delivery tests, incident ownership, escalation paths, severity definitions and evidence-backed response targets.

What to include

For support requests, include the app name, package/version where known, device/Android version, approximate time, and a description of the problem. Do not email passwords, OTPs, private keys or other authentication secrets.

Security reporting

Security reports should include reproducible facts and affected versions. Publication of a fix is governed by the release evidence process; an unverified source change is not treated as a production fix.

Abuse and privacy

Abuse, privacy, deletion and account concerns must be routed through reviewed production workflows before public launch. The Store must not claim completed handling where the required workflow has not been tested.
