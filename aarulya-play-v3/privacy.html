Privacy Policy — Aarulya Play

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Current product boundary: Aarulya Play is an original skill-game experience. It does not provide deposits, wagering, cash stakes, cash win/loss, or withdrawals.

Data stored on the device

The current three-game build stores virtual coin balance, high scores, completed-game counts and mission progress in local browser/app storage on the device.

Advertising and consent

The Android build can include Google Mobile Ads. Advertising is requested only after the app's consent flow permits it. Development APKs use Google test ad identifiers and do not generate publisher revenue. Production ad identifiers must be supplied separately before a commercial release. Ads are designed for the lobby or natural game breaks; active gameplay is not intentionally interrupted.

Cloud features

Future account, leaderboard, analytics or synchronization features may use Aarulya-controlled cloud services. Those features must be documented here before production activation and must not silently convert local gameplay data into a cash or wagering system.

Children and age treatment

Aarulya Play is being prepared with age-aware and family-safe advertising controls. Commercial launch remains subject to the final target-audience, parental-control and child-privacy review required for the chosen markets and app stores.

Security

The Android app disables cleartext network traffic and keeps the game assets bundled locally. Production signing keys, advertising credentials and server secrets are not stored in the game source repository.

Your choices

You can clear the app's local data from Android system settings to remove local progress. Any future account-based export or deletion workflow will be documented before that feature is made public.

Contact

Use the contact channel published on the official Aarulya website for privacy or support requests.
