# Child-friendly game expansion

This specification expands Aarulya Play from 20 to 30 original game concepts while keeping the platform suitable for family and child profiles.

## New game modules

### Aarulya Craft World
- Engine: sandbox builder
- Core loop: collect safe materials, build themed spaces, complete creation missions
- Differentiation: Indian village, festival, science-lab and eco-city themes
- Safety: curated objects, no public user-generated uploads, no open stranger chat

### Aarulya Obstacle Party
- Engine: party platformer
- Core loop: short obstacle rounds, checkpoints and bot races
- Differentiation: original mela, monsoon, rooftop and toy-world courses
- Safety: non-violent elimination and expressive emotes only

### Aarulya Pet Town
- Engine: pet simulation
- Core loop: feed, clean, decorate, train and complete kindness missions
- Differentiation: original fantasy pets and Indian habitat themes
- Safety: no real-money pet trading or public messaging

### Aarulya Farm Builder
- Engine: casual simulation
- Core loop: plant, harvest, craft, decorate and fulfill village orders
- Differentiation: seasonal Indian crops and water-saving missions
- Safety: age-appropriate timers and no pressure-based purchases

### Aarulya Cooking Rush
- Engine: time management
- Core loop: prepare orders, sequence actions and improve kitchen stations
- Differentiation: original regional-food-inspired fictional recipes and festival kitchens
- Safety: no unsafe cooking instructions or external chat

### Aarulya Fashion Studio
- Engine: customization
- Core loop: combine colours, patterns and accessories for design challenges
- Differentiation: original outfits and textile-inspired patterns without copying brands
- Safety: child-appropriate avatars and no body-rating mechanics

### Aarulya Drawing Quest
- Engine: creative drawing
- Core loop: trace, draw, colour and solve visual prompts
- Differentiation: animals, nature, space and Indian culture learning packs
- Safety: on-device drawings by default and no public image upload in child mode

### Aarulya Math Adventure
- Engine: learning puzzle
- Core loop: arithmetic, logic, shapes and timed level challenges
- Differentiation: adaptive difficulty and Hindi/English instructions
- Safety: no public leaderboard for young-child profiles

### Aarulya Hide & Seek Party
- Engine: party stealth
- Core loop: hide, search, collect clues and escape within short bot matches
- Differentiation: toy house, garden, school-fair and space-station maps
- Safety: bots/local trusted play first and no stranger voice chat

### Aarulya Monster Merge
- Engine: merge puzzle
- Core loop: combine friendly creatures, complete collection goals and unlock habitats
- Differentiation: entirely original creatures, names, animations and progression
- Safety: no loot boxes, wagering or purchasable random rewards

## Shared quality requirements

- Fast startup and low-end Android support
- Short 1–5 minute sessions with pause/resume
- Hindi, English and Hinglish text support
- Original art, audio, level layouts and characters
- Clear tutorial before each game
- Haptic and sound controls with mute option
- Parent mode, play-time reminders and age-aware defaults
- Bot or local trusted multiplayer before public matchmaking
- No open chat, stranger direct messages or cash prompts for child profiles

## Revenue boundaries

The new modules may support ads between sessions, subscriptions, cosmetic themes, sponsored educational challenges and licensing. User deposits, wagering, cash win/loss and withdrawable virtual coins remain disabled.
