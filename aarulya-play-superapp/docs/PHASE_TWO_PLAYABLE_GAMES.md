# Phase Two Playable Games

This milestone adds five additional browser-playable game foundations to the common Aarulya battle shell.

## Modules

### Aarulya Block Puzzle
- Six-by-six board
- Multiple piece shapes
- Row and column clearing
- Timed player-versus-bot score result

### Aarulya Goal Master
- Nine selectable goal zones
- Five penalty shots
- Independent goalkeeper and bot scoring
- Win, loss or draw receipt

### Aarulya Color Dash
- Hindi colour prompts
- Six selectable colour tiles
- Correct-answer and wrong-answer scoring
- Forty-five-second reflex battle

### Aarulya Memory Battle
- Sixteen cards and eight pairs
- Match state and completion bonus
- Timed bot score pressure
- Child-friendly symbols with no stranger interaction

### Aarulya Bubble Arena
- Seven-by-seven colour grid
- Connected-group detection
- Group-size scoring
- Gravity/refill cycle and timed bot comparison

## Shared requirements

- Uses the same BattleEngine profile, XP, rank and virtual-coin receipt.
- Contains no cash stake, deposit, withdrawal or wager.
- Uses original Aarulya names and source implementation.
- Final art, sound and music must be original or properly licensed and recorded in the asset register.
- Browser and low-end Android WebView testing remain required before release.
