# Aarulya Play Monetization Foundation

## Current state

No live ad provider is connected. The source defines placement, audience and revenue-allocation rules only. Production ad serving requires a separately approved provider account, test ads, privacy disclosures, consent handling, age treatment and release verification.

## Provider responsibility

Aarulya owns the publisher account and receives provider settlements. The provider chooses eligible advertiser campaigns based on its inventory and policy controls. Aarulya must block unsuitable categories and must not manually promise a particular advertiser or revenue amount.

## Permitted ad moments

- Level or match result screen
- Optional rewarded placement selected by the player
- Adult/parent lobby banner after approval

## Blocked ad moments

- Immediately on app launch
- During active gameplay
- Forced in the middle of a match
- Attached to pause, close or error controls
- Any cash/referral promotion inside child mode

## Audience defaults

Unknown or unselected audience defaults to child-safe treatment.

### Child

- Contextual inventory only
- Level-end placement only
- Maximum three interstitial opportunities per day
- Rewarded placement requires parent-approved product design
- No cash referral visibility

### Teen

- Contextual inventory only
- Level-end and optional rewarded placements
- Maximum five interstitial opportunities per day
- No cash referral visibility

### Adult / Parent

- Contextual-by-default design
- Level-end, optional rewarded and lobby-banner placements
- Maximum eight interstitial opportunities per day
- Cash referral remains disabled until a fully funded campaign passes all gates

## Self-funded revenue rule

Money cannot enter the reward pool from an estimate, impression counter or unpaid dashboard balance. Only verified and settled net income can be allocated after fees and taxes.

Default allocation:

- 55% operations
- 20% game content
- 10% referral campaigns
- 10% marketing
- 5% reserve

## Cash referral activation gates

All conditions must be true:

1. Adult-only campaign
2. Published campaign terms
3. Identity, age and fraud-control workflow
4. Settled money available in the referral allocation bucket
5. Requested campaign budget does not exceed the funded bucket
6. Owner approval and complete audit trail

Until those controls exist, sharing can grant only virtual coins or non-cash cosmetic rewards.

## Revenue source order

1. Contextual ads at natural breaks
2. Ad-free subscription
3. Cosmetic themes and avatars
4. Sponsored non-wagering challenges
5. White-label or institutional licensing

The product must prioritize retention and safe gameplay over aggressive ad volume.
